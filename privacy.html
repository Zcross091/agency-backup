Privacy Policy

Effective: October 2026 • Version 1.0
01 Introduction

Northex Marketing & Advertising Agency ("Northex", "we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal and business information you share with us. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, store, utilize, and protect your information when you visit northexmarketing.com or engage our performance advertising services.

02 Information We Collect

We collect information in the following ways:

  • Directly Provided Information: When you complete our consultation or inquiry form, we collect your full name, telephone/mobile number, email address, brand or business name, target country, and project requirements.
  • Campaign Engagement Data: Client marketing assets, campaign objectives, ad account IDs, and performance analytics shared during our working partnership.
  • Technical & Analytical Data: Anonymized server logs, browser specifications, operating system details, device type, and interaction metrics to ensure optimal website delivery and security.
03 How We Use Your Information

We utilize the collected data strictly for lawful and professional agency purposes:

  • To evaluate your business model and conduct requested Growth Audits and strategy consultations.
  • To establish and manage targeted Meta and Google ad campaigns on your behalf.
  • To compile and deliver transparent weekly performance reports.
  • To communicate operational updates, invoices, and service optimizations.
  • To maintain the security, integrity, and performance of our web infrastructure.
04 Zero Data Selling Policy

We never sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal or business data to third-party data brokers or marketing vendors. Information is shared solely with necessary technical infrastructure providers (such as Google Workspace, Vercel hosting, and secure internal lead dispatch tools) exclusively to execute agreed agency services.

05 Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Our website utilizes minimal, essential cookies and analytics tags to maintain security, retain session preferences, and evaluate website performance. You have full freedom to manage or disable cookies through your browser settings without restricting core access to our site.

06 Data Security & Storage

We implement industry-standard technical and organizational security protocols, including HTTPS encryption in transit, strict access control, and secure private vault storage, to safeguard your data against unauthorized access, disclosure, or accidental destruction.

07 Your Rights & Choices

Depending on your jurisdiction (including GDPR and international data standards), you hold the right to:

  • Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Request rectification of any incorrect or incomplete information.
  • Request complete deletion or erasure of your personal details from our active intake systems.
  • Withdraw consent for direct marketing or consultations at any time.
08 Policy Revisions

We may periodically update this policy to reflect operational, legal, or regulatory advancements. Any modifications will be posted directly to this page with an updated effective date.

Data Privacy Inquiries & Requests

For data access, correction, or deletion requests, contact our privacy desk.
